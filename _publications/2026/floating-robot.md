abstract: >-
  Soft floating robots are a new form of embodied intelligence with particular advantages indoors. Compared with conventional rotorcraft, they can approach people with less noise and a lower risk of collision while making use of the vertical space in large indoor environments. This project examines how people and floating robots coordinate and collaborate during indoor inspection tasks.
